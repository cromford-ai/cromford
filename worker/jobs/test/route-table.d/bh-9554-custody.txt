# BH-9554 (custody C0): the ticket custody table. A lane claims a ticket
# (owner + lease), renews, releases it to the custodian, or sets the next
# action and deadline; settle ends the row; act is the custodian's CAS step.
# due, row and gaps are the reads. Bearer-gated.
POST /api/custody/claim
POST /api/custody/renew
POST /api/custody/release
POST /api/custody/next
POST /api/custody/settle
POST /api/custody/act
GET /api/custody/due
GET /api/custody/row
GET /api/custody/gaps
