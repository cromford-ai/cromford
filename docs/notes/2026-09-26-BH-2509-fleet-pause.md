# Fleet-scope PAUSE from Mission Control, and every pause says why, who and when

Doc: fleet-pauses

## What changed

- The Fleet page (`/`) has a `PAUSE FLEET…` button. Its modal takes WHAT,
  REASON and TTL (seconds), all required, and sends
  `pause_set {scope:"fleet", what, reason, ttl_s}` through the existing console
  action. The hub's poller already turned that into a `scope=fleet` row, and the
  merge sweep already honoured a fleet `merges` row. Neither changed.
- END on the PAUSES table and RESUME on `/p/<project>` now ask for a reason and
  stay blocked until one is typed. The reason rides on the command body. The
  `pauses` table has no end-reason column, so the commands feed is its record.
- The Pages function in front of `/api/dash/action` answers 400 and forwards
  nothing for `pause_board`, `resume_board` or `pause_set` without a non-blank
  reason, and for a fleet `pause_set` without a whole-number TTL above 0.
  `pause_end` and every other command pass as before.
- Every paused board shows its reason, actor and time: the FLAGS tag titles on
  `/`, a created-at hover on the PAUSES AGE cell, and pause lines under the
  `/p/<project>` header. A `console` actor reads
  `console (no verified identity)`. A flag-file pause with no row on record says
  exactly that rather than inventing a reason.

## Why

The 2026-09-25 audit (A8) found no way to pause the whole fleet from the
console, and pauses and resumes going on record with no reason. the operator's
2026-09-26 comment added the required reasons (modal and route), the verified
actor, and who/why/when on every paused board.

## What it does not do

- Telegram and the MCP tool do not pass through the Pages route, so the poller
  still records "paused from console, no reason given" for a reasonless
  `pause_board` from there. That is on purpose: the emergency brake must not
  become a no-op.
- The actor on a console-issued pause row is still `console` until
  harness-console's `pauseCommand()` carries the stamped actor through (#2612,
  another repo). The UI labels that honestly instead of showing a person.
