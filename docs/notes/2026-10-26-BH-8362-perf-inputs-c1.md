## What changed

- `lgsc_run` (`bin/lib/lead-gates-selfcheck.sh`) read only the body of the
  linked issue. It did not pass `PSC_TICKET_TITLE`, `PSC_TICKET_LABELS`,
  `PSC_TICKET_REPO` or `PSC_TICKET_N` to `psc_check`. The enqueue did
  (BH-6380). The enqueue has no launch since BH-7235, so the perf trigger of
  `body-outcome-replay` did not fire for a ticket that is perf by its label,
  its title or its one parent hop. The worklist requested a lead review for
  that PR with no hold.
- `lgsc_run` now keeps the issue JSON in `$(lgs_dir)/psc-ticket-<slug_>-<n>.json`
  and passes the title, labels, repo and number. A failed read leaves all 4
  empty, as in the enqueue. The check still runs and the perf rule gives a NOTE.
- The parent hop adds at most 1 GitHub REST read per PR head per tick, only
  when the ticket is not perf by itself and names a parent.
