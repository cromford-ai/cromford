# Disk guard: alert at 85%, refuse to start a build at 92%

Doc: OPERATOR-SETUP, BUILD-CONCURRENCY

## What changed

- New lane `disk-guard` (`bin/disk-guard.sh`, `*/10`, every board box and the
  hub). It reads `df -P /` and raises `disk-high-<host>` (warn) at or over
  `DISK_ALERT_PCT` (85) and `disk-refuse-<host>` (critical) at or over
  `DISK_BUILD_REFUSE_PCT` (92) through `bin/lane-alert.sh`, and resolves each
  when the disk is back under.
- New `# >>> disk gate` block in `bin/run-build.sh`, after hub-no-build: at or
  over `DISK_BUILD_REFUSE_PCT` a build answers
  `BUILD_DEFERRED disk / at <pct>% on <host> (builds refused at <n>%, knob disk-guard DISK_BUILD_REFUSE_PCT); <free> MB free`
  and exits 3 before any plan or claim. It is re-offered next tick. Fly pool
  machines (`BUILDER_EXEC=1`) are not gated.
- Both thresholds are knobs on the `disk-guard` lane, resolved by `lane_knob`
  (settings row > env > registry default).

## Why

BH-1123, scoped by the operator on 2026-09-23: "Build only the guard: alert at 85% disk
and refuse to start a build at 92%, on board boxes and the hub. Both thresholds
should be lane-registry knobs." The one-off sweep in the issue body was dropped.

## Operator notes

- A df with no reading is logged once an hour to `logs/disk-guard.log` and does
  nothing else. The gate fails open on it.
- `flags/lane-off-disk-guard` stops the watcher, not the gate. To stop refusing
  builds set `DISK_BUILD_REFUSE_PCT` to 100.
