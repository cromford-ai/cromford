# Merge-queue shards past the 45 min job cap: shard sizing, a concurrency report, and no strike for a no-verdict ejection (BH-7620)

Doc: ci-durations, CI-RUNNERS, MERGE-CONDUCTOR

## The incidents (2026-10-19)

Mergify ejected three batches with no failing test. Each time three batches of 12 shards ran at once.

- 19:45Z, batch #7589 (#7587): shards 4 and 8 cancelled at the cap.
- ~21:22Z, batch #7612 (#7508): shards 1, 2, 4, 5, 9, 10 and 11 cancelled.
- ~21:22Z, batch #7613 (#7417, #7515, #7558), run 39320354238: shards 4, 5, 7, 8 and 10 cancelled at 45.4 min.

The plan job of run 39320354238 printed:

```
ci-plan: sizing: time (artifact), 12 shard(s), slowest est 2036s of budget 480s, cap 12; over budget (biggest suite 968s); cap reached
```

The planner expected a 34 min slowest shard before any runner contention. The 480 s budget could not bind, because the 12-shard cap bound first.

## What changed

1. `bin/ci-shard-concurrency.sh` (new, read-only). It reads the last N merge-queue batch runs of `harness-ci.yml` and prints one TSV row per run: the shard walls, the number of batch runs that overlapped it (`conc`), the shards cancelled at the cap, and the plan's own estimate. The summary has one `conc <c>:` line per concurrency level. The cap is read from the `tests-shard` job's `timeout-minutes` in the workflow file.
2. Registry defaults of the `ci-plan-settings` lane: `CI_SHARD_BUDGET_S` 480 -> 1200, `CI_MAX_SHARDS` 12 -> 24. `bin/ci-plan.sh` is unchanged; it now plans the fewest shards whose slowest one is within 20 min of suite time.
3. The merge conductor's settle pass asks `nv_eject_note` (`bin/lib/mc-noverdict.sh`) before it counts strikes. A batch that failed only because shards left no verdict (cancelled at the cap, or a runner died) is cached as `nvv … noverdict`. `mc_eject_strikes` (`bin/lib/mc/queue.sh`) leaves that `ejected` line out of the count, unless the head also has a `reejected` line. The re-entry arm then re-queues each member PR once, through the BH-6336 path (`nv_gate`, the pre-queue gate, the tick budget, `mc_requeue_mergify`).

## The decision (requirement 2), with the inputs

- Options: rebalance by recorded test time (chosen); lower Mergify `max_parallel_checks` from 3 to 2 (cuts queue throughput by a third); raise the 45 min job cap (each eject then costs more runner minutes, and it hides slow shards).
- R, the slowdown under contention: at least 1.33 (5 shards of run 39320354238 were cancelled at 45.4 min = 2724 s against a 2036 s estimate). 1.33 is a lower bound: the cap cut those shards off, so their real wall is not known.
- B = 1200 s. A shard that plans 20 min stays under the 45 min cap up to a 2.25x slowdown. 1200 is under the ticket's 25 min plan ceiling (1500 s) and above the biggest suite (968 s). The plan's formula min(1500, floor(2400 / R)) gives 1500 at R = 1.33; 1200 was kept because R is censored.
- C = 24. A full batch has at most 12 x 2036 = 24432 s of suite time, which needs about 21 shards at 1200 s. 24 leaves room for corpus growth.
- Expected replay result: every batch's slowest shard estimate is at most 20 min (B / 60), under the 30 min acceptance line, while the corpus stays under 24 x 1200 s. This is arithmetic from one run's plan line, not a measured replay.
- Cost: a full batch fans out to about 21 shards instead of 12, about 9 more job setups (1-2 min each). One avoided ejection saves about 12 x 45 runner minutes and a full re-run. A small selection now gets fewer shards than 12.

## Operator steps

1. Run the concurrency report from a box that can read the GitHub API, and put its summary lines in the PR: `bin/ci-shard-concurrency.sh --last 50 --out /tmp/conc.tsv; echo rc=$?`
2. Run the replay at the new defaults: `CI_MAX_SHARDS=24 CI_SHARD_BUDGET_S=1200 bin/ci-plan-split-replay.sh --last 50 --out /tmp/replay.tsv; echo rc=$?`, then `awk -F'\t' 'NR>1 && $13 != "-" && $13+0 > m { m = $13+0 } END { print "max new_est_min", m }' /tmp/replay.tsv`. The value must be under 30.
3. If a `ci-plan-settings` settings row pins `CI_MAX_SHARDS=12` or `CI_SHARD_BUDGET_S=480`, update it to 24 and 1200. A row wins over the registry default. The hourly `bin/ci-plan-settings.sh` tick then projects the values into the repo variables.

## Rules kept

- A `reejected` line always counts. The second ejection of a re-queued head is `queue-ejected-twice`, and `nv_twice_note` sends one crit alert, as in BH-6336.
- An unread batch at the settle tick counts as a strike (`EJECTED_NOVERDICT_UNREAD`). `nv_eject_note` writes no `nvunread` line; that counter stays `nv_gate`'s.
- A real test failure in any shard next to a cancelled one is `real`: a strike, no requeue.
- `MC_EJECT_REBASE=0` keeps the old count, with no exemption.
- Heads already parked `queue-ejected-twice` before this change stay parked; nothing is re-queued after the fact.
