# The /tmp sweep removes builder and reviewer checkouts, every 15 min (BH-5608)

Doc: tmp-sweep

## What changed

`bin/tmp-sweep.sh` (BH-5007) is the same lane with a wider scope and a faster
cadence:

- The default `TMP_SWEEP_PATTERNS` adds `rv* wt* r[0-9]* bh[0-9]*-* pb*-*
  lanes-build.* review-prs-*` to the BH-5007 names. The list is still not
  settable from a page.
- The `MIN_AGE_MIN` default is 180 (was 360). Min 30, unchanged.
- Cron runs it at `8,23,38,53 * * * *` (was `23 * * * *`).
- `rpool_init` in `bin/lib/review-pool.sh` runs it once, bounded at 120 s,
  before the pool reads free /tmp. Both review-prs and lead-review call
  `rpool_init`. `REVIEW_POOL_TMP_SWEEP=` (empty) turns that run off.
- A line of a file in `state/claims/<project>/<KEY>` that names a path under a
  /tmp entry keeps that entry (`skipped_held`).
- When a removed entry was a git worktree, `git worktree prune` runs once in
  the repo that owned it.
- The removal line is `removed <path> <kb> KB age_min=<n>`.
- Every real sweep writes a `box_health` ledger row, key `tmp-sweep <host>`,
  with `freed_bytes`.
- Under `HARNESS_TEST_SANDBOX=1` with no `TMP_SWEEP_ROOT`, the sweep is skipped.

## Why

On 2026-10-08 nine builder and reviewer checkouts (6 to 19 h old, up to 470 MB
each) held 1.5 GB of box-small's 4 GB /tmp. No pattern matched them.
review-prs sizes its pool from free /tmp and ran at concurrency 1 from 18:37Z
to 20:31Z, and 40-45 PRs waited for a builder re-approve.

## Operator check

If a settings row `lane.tmp-sweep.min_age_min=360` exists, it still wins over
the new 180 default. Remove the row to take the new default.

Tests: `tests/tmp-sweep.bh-5608-test.sh`, `tests/tmp-sweep.bh-5007-test.sh`.
