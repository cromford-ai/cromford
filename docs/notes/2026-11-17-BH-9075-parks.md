# BH-9075: the parks table, and the resolver and conductor delta reads

Doc: MERGE-CONDUCTOR, events-bh-9067/3-parks

Slice 4 of 5 of BH-9067 (BH-9067.3).

## Why

The resolver re-reads 2,367 state record files and the queue rows on every
tick to find its parks. The conductor walks every PR of every project on every
tick, also when nothing changed. One D1 table of parks lets both read only
what changed.

## What changed

- New D1 table `parks` (`worker/jobs/schema.d/202611150806-bh-9075-parks.sql`)
  with two indexes and six settings seeds, and four routes in
  `worker/jobs/src/routes/parks.js`: `PUT /api/parks`, `GET /api/parks/changed`,
  `GET` and `POST /api/parks/prune`.
- New client `bin/lib/jobs/parks.sh` and writers `bin/lib/parks-write.sh`.
- Writer call lines: the two queue park lines of the fly-pool reap (fragment
  `config/fly-pool.d/3400`, which also loads the lib), `br_set_outcome`, and the
  conductor tick end.
- The resolver: `br_candidates` reads `BR_PARKS_MODE` (default `shadow`). The
  new lazy module `bin/lib/br/opt/parks.sh` keeps a per-project mirror and
  cursor and writes one `parks-shadow:` line per tick.
- The conductor: `bin/lib/mc/parks-delta.sh` and two hook lines in
  `tick_project`. `MC_PARKS_DELTA_MODE` is `off` by default.

## Fix round r21 (PR #9775 merge-queue ejection)

- Over `PW_OUTBOX_MAX`, `pw_flush` writes `state/parks-outbox/.dropped-at`
  instead of deleting the resolver cursors. `_brp_cursor` reads a cursor older
  than the marker as 0 (`tests/no-state-files.bh-4159-test.sh`).
- The 3 `say` lookups in `bin/lib/br/discovery.sh` and `bin/lib/br/opt/parks.sh`
  use `declare -F say`. A `say` binary is never run.
- `PRUNE_IDS_SQL` in `worker/jobs/src/routes/parks.js` is one literal line, so
  `check:sql-command-injection` proves `PRUNE_DELETE_SQL` safe. The SQL text is
  the same.

## What did not change

- The state record files and `.failed.json` stay. They are the cache.
- `br_from_conductor` and its hand-off recovery loop run in every mode.
- `worker/jobs/src/index.js`, `worker/jobs/src/routes/retention.js`,
  `BR_OPT_MODULES` and `JC_MODULES`.

## Owed

- The conductor delta's prequeue D1 read (#9061 holds none).
- The `on` flips, after the shadow lines read clean
  (`docs/events-bh-9067/3-parks.md`, "Flip steps").
