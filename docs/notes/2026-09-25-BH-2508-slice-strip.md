# A sliced master loses ai-build, and the hotfix close asks the guard

Doc: MERGE-CONDUCTOR

## What was wrong

BH-2477 stopped the merge close-out closing a master that a merge only sliced, but it left `ai-build` on the master, so the watchers kept offering it for a build. The guard found a plan slice through a planner sentence ("The build for THIS ticket is C0") rather than a line meant to be read by a machine. And `bin/hotfix-after-deploy.sh` still closed the ticket's issue after a verified deploy without asking the guard at all.

## What it does now

A slice verdict posts `Slice delivered: #<pr>; children own the rest.` and then runs `issue_strip_ai_build`, the close path's own label rule (split-child exemption, `CLOSE_STRIP_AI_BUILD` switch). The word becomes `kept-slice:<why>+<strip word>`. The conductor and sweep strip only after their comment posted. The planner writes `SLICE: C<n> of #<ticket>` on a one-slice plan and `issue_close_guard` matches it for this issue only; the old sentence still matches as a fallback. The hotfix close-out asks the guard first: a slice is settled the same way, and an undecided or missing guard closes nothing and writes a `HOTFIX CLOSE-OUT OWED` line. Details are in `docs/verification-map/closed-issue-hygiene.md`.

Suites: `tests/issue-close-slice-strip.bh-2508-test.sh`, `tests/hotfix-close-guard.bh-2508-test.sh`.
