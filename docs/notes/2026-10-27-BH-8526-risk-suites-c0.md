# Four gate-60 suites lose their enqueue cases; three live cases move to a worklist suite (BH-8526)

Doc: TESTING

Slice C0 of #8526 (BH-8496.2). Since BH-7235 no cron lane or wake starts `bin/lead-review-enqueue.sh`. Its
risk gate runs in `bin/lead-review.sh tick` as `bin/lib/lead-gates.d/60-risk.sh`. On main at 59b0b459, 7
gate-60 suites still ran the enqueue `tick`. This slice cleans the 4 with the fewest ports: bh-5875, bh-7041,
bh-6968 and silence-before-risk. No file under `bin/`, `worker/`, `cron/` or `catalog/` changes.

## What left which suite

- `tests/lead-gates-risk.bh-5875-test.sh`: R10 went. R4 lost its comparison with the enqueue's risk-tiers body
  and now checks the gate's body by literals (the key set and each value). The enqueue closure, its token and
  `enq_tick` went.
- `tests/lead-gates-low-small.bh-7041-test.sh`: the enqueue block at the end of G3 went, with the enqueue
  closure, its token and `enq_tick`. G3's worklist checks do not change.
- `tests/risk-tier-evidence.bh-6968-test.sh`: part 3 (E5, W2, E5b, W1, W1b, W1c and its enqueue rig) went.
  Parts 1, 2 and 4 do not change. The suite has 15 checks fewer (117 to 102).
- `tests/silence-before-risk.bh-5797-test.sh`: deleted. Every case drove the enqueue.

SLIMMED.
