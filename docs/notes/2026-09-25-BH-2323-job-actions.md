# Mission Control can RETRY and REQUEUE a job

Doc: HARNESS-COORDINATION

## What changed

- The coord proxy (`console-ui/functions/api/coord/[[path]].js`) now forwards
  `POST jobs/<id>/retry` and `POST jobs/<id>/requeue`, and nothing else under
  `jobs/`. It stamps `actor: "console"` on both, the same way it stamps `hub/*`
  POSTs, so the stage event the worker writes names the console and never a
  value the browser chose.
- The Job screen (`/jobs/:id`) and the Build screen (`/builds/:id`, when the
  build joins to a job row) show RETRY and REQUEUE buttons. Each opens the
  in-app confirm, sends one POST on confirm, shows the worker's decision and
  reason, and reloads the row.
- RETRY shows only on a running job that has gone silent (stalled or quiet).
  REQUEUE shows on those and on an orphaned job. Nothing shows on a live job or
  a finished, parked or queued one.

## What did not change

- The worker routes in `worker/jobs/src/routes/job-retry.js`. The retry policy,
  the requeue cap of 3 and the double-build guard (`held`) apply to a console
  click exactly as they apply to `bin/jobs-watchdog.sh`.
- ABANDON, named in the ticket title, is not shipped. The worker has no abandon
  route, so it needs one first.
