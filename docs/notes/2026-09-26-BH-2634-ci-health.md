# Mission Control has a CI health screen with the flaky-test quarantine toggle

Doc: FLAKY-TESTS, CI-RUNNERS

## What was missing

CI minutes, runner health and the flake ledger were only readable from a box. The quarantine
tables the contract (BH-2631) added had no operator surface, so turning a quarantine on or off
meant a hand-written worker call.

## What changed

- `/ci` (nav CI) lists one row per repo: minutes over 24h, runners online/total, flakes and reds
  over 7 days counted in distinct heads, the flake rate (flaky heads / all heads with a row), and
  the active quarantine count (`console-ui/src/screens/CiHealth.js`, shaping in
  `console-ui/src/lib/ci-health.js`). A value the page could not read, or that no box pushed, is
  a "—" with a title saying which. It is never 0.
- `/ci?repo=<slug>` adds that repo's flaky tests. A test opens its quarantine change history.
  Quarantine ON/OFF goes through the in-app modal and needs a reason.
- `job:` and `workflow:` names are listed but cannot be toggled, because the worker refuses them.
  Nothing can be toggled while the quarantine read is failing, because the current state is unknown.
- The coord proxy admits one new write, `PUT metrics/flakes/quarantine` with
  `{repo, test, active, reason}`. It stamps `actor` like every other console write, drops every
  other field, and still refuses `PUT metrics/flakes` (the ledger).

## Before the sibling routes deploy

`GET /api/ci-health` (BH-2633) and the quarantine routes (BH-2632) answer 404 until they ship.
Until then the screen shows a footnote per failed read, or the error box when all three fail.
It does not crash and it does not show zeros.
