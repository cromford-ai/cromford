# Live cost rows: the cost_rows_live table, /api/cost/live, and the rollup's live block (BH-3481.2)

Doc: COST

## What changed

Child 3 of BH-3481 (ticket BH-3499). The server side of live cost streaming,
and the rollup that reads it. Nothing POSTs yet: the shell meter
(`bin/lib/cost-live.sh`) is still a stub, so on day one the GET answers empty
and the rollup writes `live.rows: 0`.

- `worker/jobs/schema.sql`: new table `cost_rows_live`, PK `(box, row_hash)`,
  indexes `cost_rows_live_run` and `cost_rows_live_upd`, and
  `cost_rows_session` on `cost_rows(session_id)`. Applied by
  `bin/harness-sync.sh` before the worker deploys.
- `worker/jobs/src/routes/cost-live.js`, right after the cost family:
  - `POST /api/cost/live {box, rows:[…]}`, at most 50 rows. One row per
    session, `row_hash = sha256("live|<box>|<session_id>")`. The row only goes
    up: a lower `cost_usd` is refused, a null never overwrites a number, an
    unpriced row can become priced, and an identical re-push changes nothing.
    `updated_at` is the worker's clock. `testproj` rows are dropped. Each POST
    prunes up to 500 rows older than 14 days. Answers
    `{inserted, updated, unchanged, as_of}`.
  - `GET /api/cost/live?[hours=24][stale_s=900][run_id=][project=]`. A row is
    settled once a `cost_rows` row carries its `session_id`, in flight when not
    settled and updated within `stale_s`, unsettled otherwise. Answers
    `{rows, in_flight_usd, unsettled_usd, settled_sessions, unpriced_rows, truncated, as_of}`.
    `rows` is capped at 500 with `truncated: true`. The sums cover every
    matching row, not the page. `in_flight_usd` is 0 when nothing is running
    and null when everything running is unpriced. `hours` and `stale_s` fall
    back to their defaults on junk and are clamped to 1..336 and 60..86400.
- `bin/cost-rollup.sh` reads `GET /api/cost/live?hours=24` and writes
  `summary["live"] = {in_flight_usd, unsettled_usd, rows (a count), truncated, as_of}`.
  A read that fails, 404s or answers the wrong shape writes
  `{"unreadable": true}` with no numbers, and one agent-rules.log line.
  `fleet-cost.csv` and `cost-per-pr.csv` do not change: they carry final rows
  only.

## Why

A long build spends for an hour before its final `cost_rows` row lands, and
nothing could see that money until then. This is the table and the read the
tripwire and the console need.
