# Archie talks in the operator's DM, and says nothing when nothing is needed (BH-4779)

Doc: OPERATOR-SETUP

## What changed

Archie's messages used to go out as triaged `fleet_alert` beats (`bin/beat.sh` →
console → `notifyProject('fleet')`), so they landed in the hub group's General
thread with the fleet alert stream. the operator never saw them there. They now go out
as ONE Telegram message each, from the bot `bin/orchestrate.sh` replies with,
through one new transport:

    bin/archie-tg-send.sh <akey> <text>

It is the only send point for:

- every check (`bin/archie.sh` `send()` → `archie_beat_gate`), follow-up-woken
  checks included;
- the held-beat digest (`archie_digest_flush`);
- the daily fleet digest (`bin/daily-digest.sh`), now Archie's daily factory
  report, signed `Archie`;
- the research lane's daily line and weekly digest (`bin/archie-research.sh`,
  `daily_beat` and `digest`);
- the learning lane's weekly report (`bin/archie-learn.sh` `deliver`).

The research lane's `kind: proposal` beat is not a message: it feeds the Ideas
screen and still goes through `bin/beat.sh`.

None of them builds a `fleet_alert` payload any more. Quiet hours and the daily
cap (`archie_beat_gate`) are unchanged; only the delivery call inside them moved.
The daily digest keeps its own send hour, its `daily_digest/<D>` ledger, its
`sent-<D>` stamp and its 3-attempt cap; its `send-failed` detail is now
`{send_rc}`.

## Where it goes

The settings row `lane.archie.archie_telegram_target` (knob
`ARCHIE_TELEGRAM_TARGET` on `catalog/lanes.d/archie.json`), shape `<chat_id>` or
`<chat_id>/<thread_id>`. A thread is sent only to a `-100…` supergroup. Blank
(the default) means the operator's DM: the chat `lib.lane-alert.lane_alert_dm_chat`
resolves to, the same one `bin/lane-alert.sh --critical` uses. A malformed value
is one warn line in `logs/archie.log` and the DM. To point Archie somewhere
else, set the row (`bin/harness-ctl.sh` or the console settings page).

No env var is added. `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` already lives in `env/global.env` on
the hub. With no token, or no target at all, the transport answers rc 2 and
writes one ALERTS line a day; the slot stays `composed` and held rows stay
`queued`, so nothing is lost.

## Exactly once

The transport reads jobs ledger `archie_tg/<akey>` before every send and answers
rc 0 with no request when it is `sent`. It writes that row `sent`, detail
`{chat, thread, message_ids, chunks, at}`, only after every chunk came back
`"ok":true`. The callers' bookkeeping is unchanged: an initiative slot is put
`sent` only after rc 0, and a failed send leaves it `composed`, resent next tick
with no model call. So a lost `sent` write is not a second message.

Two windows remain, both named rather than built around: a crash between
Telegram's ok and the ledger write resends once, and a failure on chunk 2 of a
long text resends the whole text (bodies are capped at 4000, so a send is one
chunk in practice).

The akeys: `archie:<id>:<due_at>` (a check), `archie:digest:<sha8 of the held
keys>` (the held digest), `daily-digest:<D>` (the daily report), `archie-research:daily:<day>` and
`archie-research:digest:<week>` (research), `archie-learning:<week>` (the
weekly learning report).

## No-action answers send nothing

- `catalog/prompts/archie-check.md` now says: when nothing is needed from the operator,
  leave line 2 blank.
- `archie_no_action` in `bin/lib/archie.sh` runs in `check_row` right after
  the class split and before the decision POST. A sentence that matches
  `ARCHIE_NO_ACTION_RE` and neither `ARCHIE_ASK_RE` nor `ARCHIE_NEWS_RE` closes
  the slot `empty` and advances it, with no decision row and no send. All three
  are case-insensitive EREs and settable knobs on the archie lane
  (`lane.archie.archie_no_action_re`, `lane.archie.archie_ask_re`,
  `lane.archie.archie_news_re`); their registry defaults equal the code
  defaults. Anything with an ask (a `?`, can/could you, please, should I,
  approve I<n>, your call…) is always sent. So is anything with news (red,
  failed, tripped, paused, reverted, re-queued, blocked…), even when it also
  says nothing is needed: "CI went red on three PRs; I re-queued them, no action
  needed" is sent. Only an answer whose sole content is "nothing for you" is
  silent. A malformed ask or news regex fails toward sending.
- A due follow-up with a blank note wakes nothing: its
  `archie_wake/followup:<key>` row is put `empty` and the follow-up `fired`, so
  there is no model call and no send.

## Proof

`tests/archie-dm.bh-4779-test.sh` drives the real transport, the real tick and
the real daily digest against a fake Telegram, and its C5 pins that no Archie
script (`bin/archie*.sh`, `bin/lib/archie*.sh`, `bin/daily-digest.sh`) builds a
`fleet_alert`. The research and learning sends are driven by their own suites
(`tests/archie-research-test.sh`, `tests/archie-research-scout.bh-4762-test.sh`,
`tests/archie-learning.bh-4066-test.sh`). Live probe after merge: the
`GET /api/ledger?kind=archie_tg&key=archie:<id>:<due>` row carries a
`message_id`, next to the initiative event `check:<id>:<due>` = `sent`.
