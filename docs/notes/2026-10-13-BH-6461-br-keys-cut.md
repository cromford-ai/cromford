# The resolver's ticket-key family lives only in bin/lib/br/keys.sh (BH-6461)

Doc: MODULE-MAP

BH-6461 slice C0 moved the ticket-key family out of `bin/blocked-resolver.sh` into a new module, `bin/lib/br/keys.sh`, byte for byte.
The 9 functions are `br_key_shape`, `br_key_from_branch`, `br_key_from_title`, `br_key_from_dir`, `br_ticket_key`, `br_key_source_phrase`, `br_derive_ticket_key`, `br_nokey_alert` and `br_key_in_only`. `keys.sh` is the only definition of each and is load-bearing.
The driver keeps the top-level state the family uses: `BR_TICKET_KEY`, `BR_KEY_SOURCE` and the `_BR_ONLY_REPO` memo. A module defines nothing at source time.
`BR_MODULES` is `"keys ladder record"`, and `bin/lib/br/MODULES` is regenerated in the same order. A box with the new driver and no `keys.sh` refuses the tick with one `RESOLVER MODULE MISSING: bin/lib/br/keys.sh` line.
`tests/br/expected-map.tsv` has 9 new `mod` rows. `tests/br/driver-ceiling.tsv` caps the driver at 9594 lines (9444 plus 150) and 150 column-0 `br_*` definitions.
`tests/br-split-layout.bh-6461-test.sh` (K1-K6) pins the move, the refusal, the `opt/` overlap report and, with `BR_SPLIT_BASE`, that only the key family left the driver.
5 suites that extracted key functions from the driver text now read `tests/lib/br-corpus.sh`. Their assertions and counts did not change.
Discovery (`br_from_builds`, `br_from_rebuilds`, `br_handoff_lines`) did not move. It is a later slice of #6461, with rounds, queue, conflict and the park-reason modules.
