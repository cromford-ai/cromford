# A slow read in the head-lag span is held, not paged (BH-6670)

Doc: MERGE-CONDUCTOR

## The gap

Sentry issue CROMFORD-HARNESS-Q (7791585854) has 6 `conductor-step-timeout`
events for step `head-lag`, project project-6-os. The slow call is not known.
No log of the event window is on the build box.

One `mc_step head-lag` in `pr_worker` covered six sub-calls: `mc_lane_epoch`,
`head_lag_check` (and its `head_lag_kick` close/reopen), `mc_handoff_sticky`,
`mc_batch_held` and `mc_contested_loser`. The step name could not say which
one ran slow. The call trace (BH-6462) writes a line only after a call
returns, so a call still in flight at the kill is absent (`calls: none`).
Each first stall wrote one STEP_TIMEOUT line, one warn alert and one Sentry
event, also for a read that was only slow.

This change does not fix a slow call. It makes the next event name the call.

## What changed

- The old span is five steps, in this order:
  - `lane-epoch`: `mc_lane_epoch`.
  - `head-lag`: `head_lag_check` (the branch tip read and the head-lag record read).
  - `head-lag-kick`: `head_lag_kick`, the close/reopen, set inside `head_lag_check`.
  - `handoff-sticky`: `mc_handoff_sticky` and the release paths under it.
  - `park-holds`: `mc_batch_held` and `mc_contested_loser`.
  A kill names the step that was running. The `earlier:` list of a
  WORKER_DEADLINE line times each one. The STEP_END rule is unchanged
  (`MC_STEP_LOG=1`, or a step of `MC_STEP_SLOW_S` or more).
- A step timeout in a read-only step (`lane-epoch`, `head-lag`, `park-holds`)
  is held. The worker is still killed and the row is left as it is. The
  outcome is `killed:slow-read`. One log line:
  `<key> STEP_HELD step=<s> after <n>s on <head8> (step timeout <t>s, hold <i>
  of <max>): a read-only step ran slow; worker stopped, row left <state>, the
  next tick works it again | calls: <summary>`. No lane alert. No Sentry event.
  Each hold writes one `stephold <key> <head> <step>` attempts line.
- The first `MC_STEP_HOLD_MAX` kills per (PR, head, step) are held. The next
  one pages STEP_TIMEOUT as before. A new head starts the count again.
- `head-lag-kick` and `handoff-sticky` can be mid-write at a kill. They page
  as before, and so does every other step.
- The `conductor-step-timeout` Sentry event adds the tags `pr` (the number) and
  `worker_key` (`<slug>#<n>`). The title and the log line are unchanged. The
  log line already had `<slug>#<n>` and the head.

## Knob

`MC_STEP_HOLD_MAX` (default 2, 0 = off, not a number = 2), fragment
`catalog/lanes.d/merge-conductor.knobs.d/MC_STEP_HOLD_MAX.json`. Settings row:
`lane.merge-conductor.mc_step_hold_max`. No new env var is needed.
