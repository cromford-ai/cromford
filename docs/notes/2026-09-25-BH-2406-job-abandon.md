# Mission Control can ABANDON a job, and nothing re-offers it

Doc: HARNESS-COORDINATION

## What changed

- The worker has a new terminal job status, `abandoned`, in `JOB_TRANSITIONS`
  (`worker/jobs/src/lib/core.js`). Any status a job can still leave may become
  it; `done` and `budget_stopped` cannot. Nothing moves out of it.
- `POST /api/jobs/:id/abandon` (`worker/jobs/src/routes/job-abandon.js`) is the
  only way in. It needs a `reason` and an `actor`, and in one D1 batch it sets
  the status (compare-and-set on the status it read), writes one ABANDONED
  stage event with the actor and reason, and closes the ticket's `ready` or
  `queued` build_queue rows as `failed` with an `ABANDONED by ...` reason. A
  `claimed` row is left to the pool. An abandon of an older row does not close
  a newer attempt's queue row. `PATCH /api/jobs/:id` answers 409 for
  `status: "abandoned"`. `GET /api/jobs` also filters on `ticket_id`.
- The coord proxy forwards `POST jobs/<id>/abandon` with only `reason`, and
  stamps `actor: "console"`.
- The Job and Build screens show ABANDON on stalled, quiet, orphaned, queued,
  parked and failed jobs. Its in-app dialog has a Reason box and stays disabled
  until the reason has text.
- `job_ticket_abandoned <project> <key>` (`bin/lib/jobs/jobs.sh`) answers
  whether the ticket's newest BUILD row is abandoned (a newer triage, lead-review, orc or migration row shares the key and does not count). Three consumers ask it:
  - the resume pass (`bin/lib/resume-dead.sh`) writes `ABANDONED job ...` into
    the build dir's RESULT with `.blocked-resolver-abandoned` and `COMMENTED`,
    and launches nothing;
  - the pool's `QROW requeue` (`bin/fly-pool.sh`) closes the row as
    `ABANDONED by an operator ...` and does not re-enqueue; its three callers
    write no queue file and no "requeued" line for it;
  - the blocked-resolver's `br_one` takes no rung (rc 3) and writes no record.

## Rules it keeps

- A later attempt on the same ticket opens a newer job row, and every consumer
  reads the ticket as live again.
- A worker that does not answer (`job_ticket_abandoned` rc 2) leaves all three
  consumers on their old behaviour (fail-open).
- ABANDON does not stop a running process and does not close a PR. A build
  still running after the click gets a 409 when it tries to close its row.
