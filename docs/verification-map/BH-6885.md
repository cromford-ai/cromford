# Lane-run fleet: several starts per tick, and claims that prefer warm clones (BH-6885)

**What it is:** the hub tick starts as many lane-run machines as the queued
work needs, up to a cap per tick. A machine's claim prefers the projects it
keeps warm clones of. Details: [the note](../notes/2026-10-15-BH-6885-lane-fleet-multistart.md).

- Tick (`bin/lane-fleet.sh tick`): reads `GET /api/lane-runs/stats?run_p50=1`.
  WORK = the sum over queued lanes of `queued x run_p50_s`. `lf_decide` prints
  `start <n> <reason>`: n = the largest of `warm_floor - live`,
  `ceil(WORK / lane_fleet.target_drain_s) - live` and 1 when the oldest wait
  is over `scale_up_wait_s`. n is capped by `lane_fleet.max_starts_per_tick`
  and by the room under `max_machines`. The in-flight gate, the failed-start
  hold and every stop rule are as in [BH-6126](BH-6126.md).
- Batch: stopped Fly machines are reused first (id order), then
  `machine run`. The first failure ends the batch:
  `start batch stopped after <k> of <n>: <why>`.
- Log: `tick ... action=start <mid>[,<mid>...] <reason> cost=w/a work=<s>s desired=<n>`.
- Claim (`POST /api/lane-runs/claim {machine, prefer}`): the oldest live row
  of a preferred project, unless a row waited over
  `lane_run.prefer_max_wait_s` (0 = no preference). A bad `prefer` is a 400.
- Worker (`bin/lane-run-worker.sh`): the clone cache is
  `${REPOS_ROOT:-/root/repos}`. The worker sends its newest
  `lane_run.clone_cache_n` clones as `prefer`. Only with `LANE_RUN=1` does it
  evict the rest: `clone cache: evicted <name> (keep <n>)` in
  `logs/lane-run-worker.log`.

**How to drive it:**

| what | how |
|---|---|
| the tick, the claim and the worker, over the real jobs worker and a stub flyctl | `HARNESS_TEST_SANDBOX=1 bash tests/lane-fleet-multistart.bh-6885-test.sh` |
| the routes and the query plans alone | `node tests/lib/lane-runs-claim-prefer.bh-6885.mjs` (after `bin/lanes-build.sh --write`, `bin/llm-sites-build.sh --compose` and `bin/context-budgets-build.sh --compose`) |
| the last ticks on the hub | `grep 'tick wait=' /root/harness/logs/lane-fleet.log \| tail -n 5` |
| the run p50 on a live worker | `curl -sS -H "authorization: Bearer $HARNESS_SECRET" "$JOBS_URL/api/lane-runs/stats?run_p50=1"` |
