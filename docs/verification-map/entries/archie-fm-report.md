| Archie daily factory report and stop-the-line beat (BH-4588): the hub lane `archie-fm-report` sends yesterday's report once a day and ONE Telegram beat per stop-the-line incident | [entries/archie-fm-report.md](entries/archie-fm-report.md) | `bin/archie-fm-report.sh` (cron `cron/lanes/729-archie-fm-report.cron`, `:03/:18/:33/:48` behind `hub-lease.sh check`; registry `catalog/lanes.d/archie-fm-report.json`). `archie-fm-report.sh render <YYYY-MM-DD>` prints the report text and sends nothing; `archie-fm-report.sh stl --dry-list` prints the due stop-the-line incidents (first_seen epoch, `<alert-id>@<epoch>`, class, age min, alert id, line) and sends nothing. Reads `GET /api/archie/factory-report?day=` (`worker/jobs/src/routes/archie-factory.js`), the open alerts (`alerts_list`, states new/triaged/parked) classified by `catalog/archie-stop-the-line.tsv`, and the `archie.report.*` / `archie.stl.*` settings rows. Sends only through `bin/archie-tg-send.sh` with akeys `fm-report:<day>` and `stl:<alert-id>@<epoch>` (the `archie_tg` ledger is the exactly-once record). Log: `logs/archie-fm-report.log` (`sent fm-report:<day> (mode <m>)`, `stl: beat stl:<incident> ...`). Off: `ARCHIE_FMR_ENABLED=0` or `flags/lane-off-archie-fm-report`; `archie.manage=off` sends no beat and the report's first line says Archie is off. Verified by `tests/archie-fm-report.bh-4588-test.sh` and `worker/jobs/test/archie-factory.bh-4588.test.js` |
