| Archie daily factory report and stop-the-line beat (BH-4588): the hub lane `archie-fm-report` sends yesterday's report once a day and ONE Telegram beat per stop-the-line incident | [entries/archie-fm-report.md](entries/archie-fm-report.md) | `bin/archie-fm-report.sh` (cron `cron/lanes/729-archie-fm-report.cron`, `:03/:18/:33/:48` behind `hub-lease.sh check`; registry `catalog/lanes.d/archie-fm-report.json`). `archie-fm-report.sh render <YYYY-MM-DD>` prints the full report text and sends nothing; `render <YYYY-MM-DD> --telegram` prints the short Telegram text the tick sends (BH-9449: what needs the operator first, one line each with its link, then mode, merge counts, spend total vs the day caps, and the Mission Control link `<console_pages_url>/factory?day=<day>`; per-build spend keys `<project>.<n>.<hex>` roll into their project in both texts) and sends nothing; `archie-fm-report.sh stl --dry-list` prints the due stop-the-line incidents (first_seen epoch, `<alert-id>@<epoch>`, class, age min, alert id, line) and sends nothing. Reads `GET /api/archie/factory-report?day=` (`worker/jobs/src/routes/archie-factory.js`), the open alerts (`alerts_list`, states new/triaged/parked) classified by `catalog/archie-stop-the-line.tsv`, the `archie.report.*` / `archie.stl.*` settings rows, and the `report.spend_min_usd` / `report.spend_cap_pct` / `report.spend_unknown_min_usd` rows (the short text's spend filter; fallbacks 5.00, 50, 1.00). Sends only through `bin/archie-tg-send.sh` with akeys `fm-report:<day>` and `stl:<alert-id>@<epoch>` (the `archie_tg` ledger is the exactly-once record). Log: `logs/archie-fm-report.log` (`sent fm-report:<day> (mode <m>)`, `stl: beat stl:<incident> ...`). Off: `ARCHIE_FMR_ENABLED=0` or `flags/lane-off-archie-fm-report`; `archie.manage=off` sends no beat and the full report's first line says Archie is off (the short text: `Mode: off (Archie took no action).`). Verified by `tests/archie-fm-report.bh-4588-test.sh`, `tests/archie-fm-report-short.bh-9449-test.sh` and `worker/jobs/test/archie-factory.bh-4588.test.js` |
